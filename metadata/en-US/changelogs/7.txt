The drawer can search your contacts (settings › drawer › search contacts, off by default; asks for the contacts permission when switched on). Tapping a contact opens it, calls or texts, as you choose; long-press for the others. Contacts stay in memory and are never saved or sent. New, also off by default: when nothing matches, the drawer offers to search Google for what you typed, in your browser. Back up all your settings and imported fonts to a file and restore them, under settings › backup.
